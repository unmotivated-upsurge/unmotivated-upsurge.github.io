the unmotivated upsurge of the worldmerleau-ponty reading group

we are a group of researchers and students gathered to read and discuss maurice merleau-ponty's work. since 2025, we hold bi-weekly meetings that take place at universiteit leiden.

if you want to get involved in the work of the group, you can sign up to our newsletter or contact us at EMAIL-HERE.
